## ⭐ Der Prompt

```
Photorealistic editorial portrait of a Swedish man in his early sixties
who has lived in Berlin for three decades. Angular face with pronounced
cheekbones and a strong jawline, a straight nose, calm alert blue-grey
eyes that look like they are weighing something. Clear lines around the
eyes and mouth, the face of someone who has sat through many difficult
conversations. Dense silver-grey hair, cut short and combed back neatly,
no styling gloss. Clean shaven. Fair skin with a cool Nordic undertone,

He wears a dark navy blazer over a plain open-collar shirt, no tie.
Muted palette only: navy, charcoal, warm grey.

Head and shoulders, three-quarter angle with the face turned slightly
toward the camera, eyes looking directly into the lens. Composed,
attentive expression without a smile. Authoritative but not cold, the
look of an advisor who listens before he answers.

Soft natural window light from the front left, gentle falloff on the
right cheek, no hard shadows, no rim light. Clean bright neutral

Shot on 85mm lens at f2.0, sharp focus on the eyes, shallow but not
extreme depth of field. Documentary editorial photography, quiet and
understated, like a portrait for a business book jacket.
```

## ⚠️ Hinweise zur Auswahl

Acht bis zwölf Varianten erzeugen und das **unauffälligste** Ergebnis nehmen, nicht das schönste.

Bei diesem Alter besonders wichtig: Falten und Hautstruktur nicht wegglätten lassen. Viele Modelle verjüngen automatisch. Wenn das Ergebnis wie Mitte vierzig aussieht, den Prompt um `deep set eyes, weathered skin, visible age` ergänzen und neu erzeugen.

Kein reales Vorbild. Der Prompt darf keinen existierenden Menschen benennen, und das Ergebnis darf niemandem erkennbar ähneln.
